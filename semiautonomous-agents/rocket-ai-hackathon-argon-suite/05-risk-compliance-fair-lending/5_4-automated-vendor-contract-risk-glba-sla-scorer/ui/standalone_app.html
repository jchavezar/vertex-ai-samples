MASTER ENTERPRISE CLOUD & AI SERVICES AGREEMENT

Between Rocket Mortgage, LLC ("Customer") and DocuCloud AI Analytics, Inc. ("Vendor")

WHEREAS, Customer is a federally regulated financial institution subject to the Gramm-Leach-Bliley Act (GLBA) Safeguards Rule (16 CFR Part 314), CFPB supervisory examination, and NYDFS 23 NYCRR Part 500; and Vendor processes Nonpublic Personal Information ("NPI") including Form 1003 loan applications, Social Security numbers, and tax transcripts.

SECTION 11.4 — CYBERSECURITY INCIDENT & GLBA BREACH NOTIFICATION SLA

11.4 Security Incident Notice. In the event Vendor discovers any unauthorized access, acquisition, or exfiltration of Customer Nonpublic Personal Information (NPI), Vendor shall notify the Rocket CISO and Legal Incident Response Desk within seventy-two (72) hours of confirming a material breach twenty-four (24) hours of initial detection of any suspected or confirmed Security Incident per NYDFS 23 NYCRR § 500.17 and FTC GLBA Safeguards Rule, and shall provide continuous root-cause forensic logs every 6 hours thereafter.

SECTION 14.1 — FOUNDATION MODEL TRAINING & ZERO DATA RETENTION COVENANT

14.1 AI Data Sovereignty. Vendor is granted a perpetual, royalty-free license to utilize de-identified and aggregated Customer mortgage documents and borrower prompts to train, fine-tune, and improve Vendor's commercial foundation models strictly covenants that Customer Data, prompts, embeddings, and borrower NPI shall NEVER be used to train, fine-tune, or distill any shared or third-party AI model. Vendor shall enforce ephemeral Zero Data Retention (ZDR) in US-only regions (us-central1 / us-east4).

SECTION 8.2 — LIMITATION OF LIABILITY SUPER-CAP & REGULATORY RIGHT-TO-AUDIT

8.2 Data Breach Super-Cap & SOC2 Audit.

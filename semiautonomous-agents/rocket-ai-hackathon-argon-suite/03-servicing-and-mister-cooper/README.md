# 3. Client Experience & Servicing (Mr. Cooper Portfolio, Escrow, Synopsis & Recapture) (`rocket-demo-servicing`)

> **Domain Theme:** Mr. Cooper x Rocket Servicing Omnichannel CX & Voice Studio (The Sierra AI Killer)  
> **Mission:** Drive 85%+ first-contact resolution across Rocket & Mr. Cooper's multi-million-loan servicing portfolio (escrow shortages, payoffs, Form 1098s, CFPB hardship) and recapture refinances with Gemini 3.8 Live API.  
> **AWS / Anthropic / Sierra Win-Back:** Replaces expensive third-party Sierra AI per-resolution fees and AWS Connect + Bedrock wrappers with native Gemini Enterprise for CX (GECX), CCAI Agent Assist (upgrading Rocket Logic Synopsis), and sub-200ms Gemini 3.8 Live API.
